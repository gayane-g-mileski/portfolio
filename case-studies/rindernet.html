01 — Context

In Austria every cattle movement — a birth, a death, a sale, a border crossing — has to be reported to the national veterinary register within seven days. Miss it and subsidies are at risk, and a disease outbreak becomes much harder to trace.

Rindernet is the country's official cattle register: roughly two million animals across more than 18,000 farms. Until 2023 you reported either on paper or through a desktop-only website last touched in 2014.

In practice, farmers filled in forms in the barn — often days after the event — then posted or faxed them to a regional office, where somebody typed them in again. Two chances to get it wrong. Reports took 4.2 days on average, and in spring, when the calves arrive, the busiest farms were regularly past six.

Brussels had set a deadline: Austria had to show better traceability by early 2025. That gave the programme a fixed date to work back from, and Rinderzucht Austria and the agriculture ministry asked us to lead it.

The whole brief in one sentence: a 65-year-old farmer, standing in a dark barn with cold hands and no signal, should be able to report a calf's birth correctly in under two minutes, first time, without ringing the helpline.

02 — Systems Mapping

Before we drew a single screen, we had to work out who all the players were — and there were fourteen of them.

A single report touches the farmer, the regional breeders' association, AMA, the ministry, vets in nine provinces, border posts, abattoirs and the EU's own animal movement system.

We mapped the whole thing across 3.2 metres of wall. The back office is genuinely complicated; the point was that the farmer should never have to know that.

03 — Accessibility

Designing for gloves, glare, older eyes and patchy signal is not a compliance box. On this product it was the strategy.

Meeting the accessibility standard was the floor, not the goal. The harder problem was where the app gets used, so we set ourselves a few barn rules and held every screen to them:

Glove-First Touch Targets
All interactive elements minimum 56×56px. Primary actions 64px+. No swipe-only interactions on critical paths.
High-Contrast Sunlight Mode
Contrast ratios 7:1+ for all text. Auto-switching high-contrast mode triggered by ambient light sensor threshold.
Large Text & Simple Language
Minimum 16px body text. Plain German (Leichte Sprache principles for key instructions). No jargon without plain-language explanations.
Full Offline Capability
All core reporting tasks work with zero connectivity. Clear offline state indicators. Zero data loss guarantee — local storage with encrypted SQLite queue.
